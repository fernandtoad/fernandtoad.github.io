Privacy Policy: FernAndToadTees Pin Tool

Last updated: 15 October 2026

The FernAndToadTees Pin Tool ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the FernAndToadTees Etsy shop to manage the shop's own Pinterest account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

When the shop owner connects their Pinterest account through Pinterest's own sign-in (OAuth), the app can access:

  • the owner's boards and the Pins saved to them;
  • Pins the app creates on the owner's account;
  • analytics for the owner's Pins (impressions, saves, clicks);
  • Pinterest Trends data (trending search terms).

The app never asks for or stores a Pinterest password.

How the data is used

  • To find which topics and Pin styles are trending in the shop's niche (gardening and nature).
  • To create and publish Pins that link to the shop's Etsy listings, after the owner approves them.
  • To measure how those Pins perform.

The text and images of Pins the owner has saved to their boards may be sent to AI model providers (such as Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter) to describe their style and themes. These providers process the data only to return a result to the app.

What is stored, and where

  • Pinterest access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer and server, in a file only the owner's account can read.
  • Pin text, images, trend data and analytics are stored on the same machines for the app's analysis.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone other than the AI model providers above.

Other people's information

The app does not collect information about other Pinterest users. It reads only Pins the owner has chosen to save to their own boards, and the public content of those Pins.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept only as long as it is useful for the shop's Pinterest work. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest's settings (Settings > Security > Connected apps), after which the app's stored tokens stop working and are deleted.
